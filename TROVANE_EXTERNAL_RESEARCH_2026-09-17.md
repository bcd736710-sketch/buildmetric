## Method and boundaries

Google result pages were requested, but their rendered result listings were not readable in the available response. Direct-page discovery was supplemented with DuckDuckGo’s public HTML results. External product specifications, testing, compatibility, packaging and safety claims were used only to identify buyer questions and market language. They were not transferred to TROVANE product facts.

## Repeated themes and search intents

1. Vehicle accessory buying requires a defined installation location, vehicle context, clearance and removal process; product dimensions alone do not prove fit.
2. “Universal” and security-style wording occur in retail listings, so a supplier page needs a clear claim boundary rather than copying that language.
3. Retractable-leash buyers repeatedly compare selected length, brake/lock operation, handle, line and configuration; this is already well covered by TROVANE.
4. Wholesale raincoat buyers care about size-chart method, sample fit, customer instructions, selected size range, colour, packaging and order confirmation.
5. Reflective and weather-oriented features need evidence-bounded wording; a design description is not a certified performance result.

## Content gap and opportunity decisions

### 1. Dog Car Window Safety Guard: product-level buyer confirmation notes

- TROVANE now has: a product page with published EVA material, dimensions and colours, plus a longer Buyer Guide.
- External signal: four readable retailer pages repeatedly frame vehicle context, installation, ventilation, fit and restrictions.
- Gap: the product page did not give a quick distinction between published facts and compatibility / installation items requiring confirmation.
- Existing-page solution: YES. A product-page Buyer Notes module is more useful and less duplicative than another guide.
- Decision: EXECUTE.

### 2. Dog raincoat wholesale sizing and sample-approval guide

- TROVANE now has: a published raincoat product with polyester, XS–2XL back/neck/chest measurements, listed colours and design descriptions.
- External signal: two current manufacturer pages use size measurement, fit, sample, colour and packaging as B2B sourcing topics.
- Gap: no TROVANE article serves the distinct wholesale sizing / sample-approval intent, and no existing blog covers this product.
- Existing-page solution: PARTIAL. The product page supplies exact model data; a dedicated guide supplies the buyer workflow without repeating the product page.
- Decision: EXECUTE as one new Buyer Guide.

### 3. Retractable dog leash selection content

- TROVANE now has: Product Buyer Notes, a Buyer Guide and a Cycle-Test Framework.
- External signal: wholesale pages repeat length, brake/lock and configuration selection.
- Gap: insufficient; current coverage already answers the same B2B task.
- Decision: REJECT — duplicate-content risk.

### 4. Foldable dog car seat with mesh safety tether

- TROVANE now has: a live product page with product facts and car-travel wording.
- External signal: vehicle-accessory listings make safety and compatibility wording commercially sensitive.
- Gap: product governance and evidence, not an ordinary SEO gap. The live page includes “safer”, “more secure” and an internal “safety tether” description without current first-party safety evidence in scope.
- Decision: REJECT / HOLD — manual product, instruction and claim review required before SEO expansion.

### 5. Dual-Use Pet Car Seat Tether

- TROVANE now has: Product Buyer Notes and a focused Buyer Checklist.
- Gap: collision / restraint evidence remains unresolved.
- Decision: REJECT / HOLD — do not create content or extend claims until product evidence and intended-use review are complete.

## Evidence limitations

- This research records live pages accessed on 2026-09-17; blocked or unreadable pages were excluded.
- Search-result snippets and competitor listings are not treated as product evidence for TROVANE.
- No external material, dimension, weight, capacity, testing, certification, MOQ, lead-time or compatibility claim was copied into TROVANE content.
- The two executed actions rely only on current TROVANE Production facts and explicit confirmation boundaries.
